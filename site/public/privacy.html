Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-04.

This page covers what Verse (verseworlds.fun) and its Valheim server collect, in plain terms.

The website

This site is a static page with no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics or tracking scripts of our own. It's hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard web server logs (IP address, request time, user agent) to serve the page and for its own abuse protection - see Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle that.

The game server

Playing on the server is inherent to multiplayer gaming: your Steam ID, in-game character name, and connection metadata (like your IP address) are visible to the server the same way they would be on any other Valheim server, and are logged for moderation purposes (banning cheaters and abusive players). We don't sell or share this data, and we don't collect anything beyond what the game itself transmits to play multiplayer.

No accounts, no payments

There is no account system and nothing is purchased through us. We don't collect email addresses, real names, or payment information.

Third parties

Social platforms we post to (YouTube, TikTok, etc.) have their own privacy policies governing anything you do there, such as commenting - this policy only covers verseworlds.fun and the game server.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact details on the site.
